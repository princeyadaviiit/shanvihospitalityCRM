## Package Filter Suggestions (for Developer)

Recommended filter UI options:
- **By Destination:** State/Country selector
- **By Duration:** 1-3 Days | 4-6 Days | 7+ Days
- **By Budget:** Budget | Mid-Range | Premium

## Standard Inclusions (Most Packages)

- Accommodation (double sharing, standard rooms)
- Breakfast and dinner daily
- Private air-conditioned cab with driver
- Fuel, toll taxes, road taxes, driver allowance
- Pick-up and drop at airport/railway station
- All applicable taxes

## Standard Exclusions (Most Packages)

- Lunch
- Airfare / train fare
- Visa fees and medical insurance
- Monument entrance fees and camera fees
- Personal expenses
- Tips, porterage, laundry, alcohol
